- **Regulation as a main area** on the check-in card, not a flag only.

- **Brain dump** is used heavily. Secondary gets it in round 8.

### Timetable maker (primary, from Azeem, 31/10/2026)
Possible tagline: "Never open Excel again." (A safer alternative: "Never open a spreadsheet again", which avoids using Microsoft's trademark.)
- **Building the week:** drag and drop blocks of time (literacy, numeracy, PE, specialists, assembly, breaks) into a weekly calendar; resize by dragging the edges. Templates: copy last week, or copy a term.
- **Counting hours:** it counts the minutes per learning area each week and term, and shows them against required hours: "Literacy 7h 30m / target 7h 30m ✓ · PE 2h / target 2h 30m ⚠". Targets are editable and set per school.
  - The exact targets need checking against the department's and the school's requirements before building (e.g. Victorian physical and sport education time, language time); don't hard-code numbers from memory.
- **Swapping:** swap or move a block for **this week only** or **permanently** (from a date). The app asks every time, the same choice a calendar gives for "this event / all events".
- **Reporting:** what was actually taught vs planned (swaps, cancellations, incursions, excursions), and whether the required hours were met for the week, term or year. Export to PDF for leadership or a coordinator.
- **Links to the rest of Radar:** the blocks become the primary "day blocks", so check-ins, events and Close day know which subject was on. The day's plan shows on Home.

## Capability snapshots
- **What:** short, regular notes per student on literacy, numeracy, digital skills and peer interactions (plus teacher-added areas), on a scale of needs support / developing / at level / above, with an optional one-line note.
- **How it's collected:** spread out, about 3–5 students a day as an extra queue card, plus a "snapshots due" step in Close day and one-tap snapshots from the brain dump. Aim to cover everyone every 3–4 weeks.
- **Seating:** could replace round 7's single "support right now" level with per-area snapshots for seating.
- **Safeguards (same as round 7):** dated, teacher-only, encrypted, never projected, shared or sent to relief teachers, and older snapshots count for less.

## Report builder
- **What:** pick a student, a date range and a report type. Radar gathers check-in trends, positives, snapshots, incidents, follow-ups, homework and assessment progress, cold call and understanding results, ICP adjustments and evidence, and notes. Wellbeing is off unless ticked.
- **Report types:**
  - semester report comments
  - parent-teacher interview notes
  - learning support / SSG meeting pack
  - handover to next year's teacher
- **Drafting:** start template-based (plain sentences, written on the device, edited by the teacher). AI-written drafts only later, with school approval and de-identified data.
- This is valuable for secondary too (semester reports). It's a good candidate for the first round after the pilot starts.
